# The harness writes research directly, with attribution

The harness may write research (Spaces, members, sources, links, reports, readings, ingest) without going through a proposal queue in Delta. Where a step needs the user's say-so, such as adding a member, the harness asks in conversation and its own tool-approval prompts apply. Every write records its author and time, and appears in the Space's activity log. The user's own records (notes, trades, premise edits, tripwires, horizon, risk appetite, archiving, renames, config) can't be written by the harness at all.

Delta can't see the conversation, so it can't verify consent; a queue would add a second approval to every action without adding safety. Research writes are reversible, and every one is attributed. See [MCP tool surface](https://github.com/lukebrown14-code/Delta/issues/66).
