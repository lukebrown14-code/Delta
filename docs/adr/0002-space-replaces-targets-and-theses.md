# Space replaces targets, themes and theses

Delta V2 organises everything around the **Space**: one investment idea, holding a premise, member instruments, notes, reports, JEV readings and trades. Watch targets, themes, theses, thesis health and the decision journal are retired rather than adapted. The premise replaces the thesis claim, JEV readings replace thesis health, and trades replace decisions.

The V1 objects each held part of an idea and made the user stitch the parts together across separate screens. A single container per idea is what the harness-first flow writes into and what the TUI shows. Instruments stay global, so a stock in two Spaces shares prices and evidence but keeps separate notes, readings and trades. See [Space model](https://github.com/lukebrown14-code/Delta/issues/60).
